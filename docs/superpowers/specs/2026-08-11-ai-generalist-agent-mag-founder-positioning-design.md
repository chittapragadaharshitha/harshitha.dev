# AI Generalist and Agent Mag Founder Positioning

## Objective

Position Harshitha Chittapragada as an AI product engineer and AI generalist who can take an operational idea from problem definition through roadmap, agent and integration design, AI-assisted implementation, deployment, and iteration. Establish her publicly as Founder of Agent Mag and make the portfolio comprehensive enough for a hiring or acquisition conversation without turning the landing page into an architecture document.

## Audience

The immediate reader is Gautam: a technically literate decision-maker evaluating Harshitha for a role and potentially evaluating AgentMag as an acquisition. The site must make three facts legible within seconds:

1. Harshitha owns and leads Agent Mag.
2. She has shipped real agent, workflow, integration, and operations systems.
3. She uses Claude Code, Codex, OpenClaw, Hermes, and related AI tools as engineering leverage, not as a substitute for technical judgment.

`AI Product Engineer · Founder of Agent Mag`

## Jarvis case study

Jarvis will be described as Harshitha's multi-agent operations assistant. The verified topology is chat or voice entry into a master agent that delegates to specialized email, calendar, and meeting agents, with memory and provider tools such as Gmail, Google Calendar, and Fireflies. Voice is an additive entry layer, not a separate duplicate orchestration system.

The portfolio will not claim that an unvalidated Vapi connector is live. It may describe voice as a designed or available entry surface only to the extent supported by the currently running Call Jarvis service and verified system evidence.
